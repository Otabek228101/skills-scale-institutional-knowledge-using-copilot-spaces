# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architecture, engineering decisions, and implementation quality across a project or workstream. They translate product intent into technically feasible solutions and ensure technical execution remains aligned with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define or validate technical architecture and design patterns
- Break down complex work into manageable technical milestones
- Review implementation quality, performance, security, and maintainability
- Identify technical dependencies, risks, and mitigation strategies
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on scope, sequencing, and trade-offs

### Goals
- Maintain a clear technical direction for the team
- Reduce avoidable delivery risk and rework
- Deliver scalable, maintainable solutions

### Typical Communication
- Architecture review meetings and technical design discussions
- Sprint planning, dependencies reviews, and escalation calls
- Coordination with developers, QA, and PMs on implementation feasibility

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to align implementation decisions and technical standards
- Partners with Product Managers to assess feasibility and prioritize trade-offs
- Supports Project Managers by flagging technical risks, critical dependencies, and delivery constraints

---

## UX Researchers / Design Partners

### Role Summary
UX Researchers and Design Partners ensure the solution addresses real customer needs and provides a usable, accessible experience. They ground product decisions in user insight and collaboration with the broader delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct interviews, usability testing, and research synthesis
- Translate customer feedback into product and workflow insights
- Partner with Product and Engineering on experience quality and design trade-offs
- Identify friction points in user journeys, onboarding, and adoption
- Help validate assumptions before major scope commitments

### Goals
- Improve customer value and usability
- Reduce misalignment between product intent and user experience
- Support confident product decisions with evidence

### Typical Communication
- Discovery workshops and validation sessions
- Product backlog refinement and customer insight reviews
- Collaboration with PMs, Designers, and Developers on user flows and edge cases

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Product Managers on user needs, priorities, and validation outcomes
- Collaborates with Developers on edge cases, accessibility, and implementation constraints
- Informs Project Managers on any user-impacting risks or dependency needs across milestones

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the project from operational, data, and regulatory risk. They ensure the team makes safe decisions during design, implementation, and release.

### Responsibilities
- Review system design for security and compliance implications
- Identify risks related to data handling, access control, and privacy
- Define required controls, review checkpoints, and remediation guidance
- Coordinate with stakeholders on policy, audit, and incident readiness
- Support release and deployment reviews with risk-based guidance

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk for the project
- Maintain confidence in release readiness and operational safety
- Embed secure practices into delivery rather than treating them as afterthoughts

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings, architecture checkpoints, and risk triage
- Release approval discussions and compliance sign-offs
- Escalation paths for incidents, vulnerabilities, and policy exceptions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Technical Leads and Developers on secure implementation patterns
- Advises Product and Project Managers on risk impact, remediation priorities, and release constraints
- Coordinates with stakeholders and leadership on high-risk issues or regulatory obligations

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help teams define success metrics, monitor outcomes, and turn operating data into evidence for decision-making. They connect delivery work to measurable business impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define metrics, dashboards, and instrumentation needs
- Align measurement plans with project goals and stakeholder expectations
- Review event tracking and reporting quality before launch
- Interpret product data to identify trends, issues, and improvement opportunities
- Support post-release evaluation and ongoing optimization

### Goals
- Make value and performance visible to the team and stakeholders
- Improve decisions using evidence rather than assumptions
- Support continuous improvement after release

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews, experiment planning, and milestone reporting
- Cross-functional analysis sessions with PMs, Product, and leadership
- Reporting updates tied to roadmap and release milestones

### Interaction with existing roles
- Helps Product Managers define and track success metrics
- Partners with Developers and Technical Leads on instrumentation and data quality
- Provides Project Managers and stakeholders with objective signals on progress and outcomes

---

## Release Managers / Delivery Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Managers or Delivery Coordinators ensure safe, predictable delivery of planned work into production. They coordinate readiness, communication, and rollback planning across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release schedules, dependencies, and readiness checkpoints
- Coordinate validation across engineering, QA, and support teams
- Track deployment risk, rollback options, and communication needs
- Ensure release notes, stakeholder updates, and operational readiness are prepared
- Support incident response and post-deployment verification for high-impact changes

### Goals
- Reduce delivery risk and avoid preventable outages
- Make releases predictable and well-communicated
- Improve coordination across teams and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Release planning, readiness reviews, and deployment check-ins
- Stakeholder communications before and after release
- Incident coordination and rollback decision support

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Project Managers to sequence milestones and deployment timing
- Works with Technical Leads and Developers to confirm release readiness
- Keeps Product Managers and stakeholders informed of release status, risk, and customer impact

---

## Business Analysts / Stakeholder Managers

### Role Summary
Business Analysts and Stakeholder Managers translate business context, operational needs, and stakeholder priorities into project requirements and alignment actions. They help ensure project decisions remain connected to business value and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify requirements from stakeholders and subject matter experts
- Translate business needs into actionable scope, acceptance criteria, and priorities
- Identify cross-functional dependencies and stakeholder concerns early
- Support communication and decision-making across business and delivery teams
- Help manage expectations around scope, trade-offs, and delivery timing

### Goals
- Keep project work aligned to business goals and stakeholder needs
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and decision-making
- Improve change management and stakeholder confidence

### Typical Communication
- Requirement workshops, stakeholder check-ins, and decision summaries
- Status updates for business stakeholders and governance groups
- Backlog discussions with Product and Project teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to refine requirements, priorities, and success criteria
- Supports Project Managers with planning clarity, dependency tracking, and communication needs
- Provides context to Developers and Technical Leads to ensure implementation aligns with business intent

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles can be added or combined depending on the size, complexity, and maturity of a project.
